How to Stop the Tremors

You are always in control. To soften or stop the shaking at any time:

  • Straighten your legs out flat along the floor.
  • Sit up, or get up and walk around.
  • Press your feet firmly into the ground.
  • Breathe slowly and relaxed.
  • Drink water, or splash a little on your face.

New to TRE? Keep shaking to a maximum of 15 minutes, 2–3 times a week.

Qi Gong

Gentle movement, breath, and awareness practice that pairs beautifully with TRE — it calms the nervous system and supports natural tremor release.

What is Qi Gong?

Qi Gong (“life-energy practice”) is an ancient Chinese discipline combining slow, flowing movement, coordinated breathing, and focused awareness. It is excellent before or after TRE to ground and settle the body.

  • Benefits: reduces stress, lowers blood pressure, improves balance & flexibility, and supports deep relaxation.
  • Best time: morning (to energize) or evening (to wind down). A few minutes is enough.
  • Pairing with TRE: a short Qi Gong warm-up primes the body; a closing practice helps integrate after tremoring.

Start with 5–10 minutes. Consistency matters more than duration.
